# Names and logos

The [MIT License](LICENSE) covers byndery's code: you may use, change and share it. It does not cover these names and marks, which identify the official apps and the account behind them:

- **Holarky**, and the Holarky mark (the leaning h with an outlined leg);
- **byndery** and the byndery icon (the leaning b whose outlined page has not closed onto the stem);
- **Phevere**, **knowrld** and their icons.

## What you may do

- Say what your work is based on, for example "a fork of byndery".
- Share unmodified official builds under their own names.
- Use the names to refer to the official apps, for example in articles, reviews and teaching.

## What a modified version must do

- Use a different name and icon, so people do not take it for the official app.
- Use its own account settings, or none, once byndery signs in to the Holarky account.
- Not suggest that Holarky made, checked or supports it.

## Asking

For anything else, such as using a name or mark in a product or a partnership, ask [thd2020](https://github.com/thd2020) first.
